# Successor Product Identity

Status: active product premise. `CANONICAL_CONTEXT.md` owns architecture;
`ART_DIRECTION.md` owns visual execution.

## Premise

Successor is a persistent social survival world built around long travel,
dangerous encounters, practical settlements, player equipment, resource
discovery, crafting, trade, farming, and recovery after defeat.

People occupy a landscape shaped by ancient structures they cannot reproduce.
Human life is improvised at a smaller scale: camps, tools, farms, extractors,
medical equipment, weapons, and trade networks made from what the world yields.

## Player promise

- The world is continuous enough for distance, routes, and landmarks to matter.
- Combat is readable and server-authoritative, with room to choose, flee,
  recover, and prepare.
- Resources differ by origin and properties; crafted objects remember the
  material and decisions that made them.
- Equipment, professions, farms, trade, groups, and settlements create reasons
  for players to depend on one another.
- Defeat changes the situation without ending the character's story.
- The graphical and terminal clients expose the same world and authority.

## Tone

Use practical, dry, worn, strange, social, and occasionally funny. People name
things for use, superstition, or local habit. Settlements should feel occupied
and maintained rather than staged for a quest.

Avoid generic military futurism, pristine space-opera surfaces, decorative
technobabble, heroic exposition, and copy that explains systems before the
world demonstrates them.

## System identity

The game should be approachable at the command level and deep in consequence.
Movement, combat, inventory, resources, crafting, farming, trade, and life state
resolve on the server. Client presentation makes those outcomes legible without
inventing them.

Loot supplies useful objects, currency, schematics, components, and materials.
It does not erase the role of gathering and crafting. Finished equipment can
carry variant stats, material history, condition, maker identity, and economic
history as those systems mature.

## World identity

The default open desert and Verdance forest establish the range: exposed
travel, sparse human footholds, wildlife ecologies, rogue danger, extractable
resources, farms, and structures large enough to orient a journey. Future areas
should extend those systemic relationships instead of becoming disconnected
theme parks.

The visual signature is the contrast between scavenged human construction and
monumental ancient work. Exact modeling, material, camera, UI, creature, crop,
and effect rules live in `ART_DIRECTION.md`.

## Content standard

A model, effect, sound, recipe, item, or system becomes part of the player
promise only when the supported runtime selects it and a focused proof covers
the path. Source and catalog work may be preserved without being described as
finished play.
