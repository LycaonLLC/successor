The repository-level MIT license applies to source code and documentation. It
does not grant a blanket license to the game assets.

Unless a nearby provenance record or license file says otherwise, models,
textures, animation, audio, video, fonts, branding, narrative content, and
other game assets in this repository are Successor project assets and remain
all rights reserved. Public visibility is not permission to extract, resell,
train on, sublicense, or reuse an asset outside the Successor project.

Third-party material remains governed by its original license. Keep every
nearby provenance record and license notice with the corresponding asset.
Font-specific terms are recorded in
`site/public/fonts/LICENSES.txt`.

Authorized Successor contributors and project partners may use these assets
for Successor work under their separate contributor or partner agreement.
Questions about asset rights should be raised with Lycaon LLC before an asset
is copied, replaced, or redistributed.
